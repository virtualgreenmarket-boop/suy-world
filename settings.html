3. Fair Play

Players must play fairly and honestly. The following actions are not allowed:

  • Using cheats, hacks, bots, scripts, modified apps, or unauthorized software.
  • Exploiting bugs or technical errors to gain an unfair advantage.
  • Selling, buying, or trading accounts outside the official game system.
  • Manipulating the game economy, rankings, rewards, or other players.
  • Trying to access another player's account.
  • Using multiple accounts to abuse rewards, promotions, voting systems, or other game features.

If you find a bug or error in the game, you should report it to the game team and not use it for personal advantage.

4. Player Behavior

Players must treat each other with respect. The following behavior is not allowed:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or abusive language.
  • Hate speech, racism, discrimination, or offensive content.
  • Sexual, violent, or inappropriate messages or images.
  • Impersonating another player, seller, moderator, or game team member.

  • Sharing personal information of yourself or others, such as phone numbers, addresses, passwords, or private photos.

The game team may remove messages, content, rooms, items, or accounts that violate these rules.

The game team may review, edit, hide, suspend, or remove any room or content that violates these rules.
